How to PlayNew here? Start with this.

What this is

A live, shared Ranked leaderboard for our DMZ crew. You log what you did after each raid, and the board instantly works out your SR (skill rating), your rank, and where you sit against everyone else. It runs in two-week seasons all the way to MW4 — climb the ranks, chase trophies, and try not to end up on the Hall of Shame. It's an honor system, so everything you enter is trusted — and everything is out in the open so the crew can keep each other honest.

Getting started

1

Finish a raid, then open “Log a Raid”

Pick your operator name from the list (or add it if you're new). Works great on your phone — no app to install, just open the link.

2

Enter what you did

Operator kills, whether you exfil'd, weapon case, contract, boss kills — and tick “ran with a squad” if you weren't solo. The scoreboard shows your SR change live as you type, so you can double-check before submitting.

3

Hit Log — that's it

Your SR, rank and placement update instantly for everyone. A quick recap popup shows what you earned and any rank-up. Got a big run? Attach a screenshot as proof to earn a ✓ Verified stamp.

Your PIN Protects your name

The first time you log, you'll set a 4-digit PIN. That locks your operator name to you, so nobody else can log raids (or mess with your score) under your name. Punch it in each time you log from that device.

New season = re-enter it

When a fresh season starts you'll get a popup and be asked to set/enter your PIN again — it just re-links your name on the new board.

Forgot it?

No stress — an admin can reset your PIN so you can set a new one. Just ask in the chat.

How your score works

You earn SR for operator kills, exfils, weapon cases, contracts, final exfils and (in Season 2) boss kills, plus one-time milestone bonuses. Every raid also charges a small deployment fee based on your current rank — the higher you climb, the more it costs to deploy, so staying on top takes real runs. Your SR never drops below zero, and your rank always follows your current SR (yes, you can get demoted). The exact numbers for everything live in the Field Manual tab.

The honor system — and how we keep it clean

Every stat is self-reported, so the board is built to make padding obvious. Play it straight and you've got nothing to worry about.

Everything is compared

Your stats show next to the squad and community average in the Leaders tab, so a genuine standout is easy to tell from someone inflating numbers.

Big-raid holds

There's a max SR (and max op kills) per raid. Go over it and your raid waits for a quick admin check before it counts — you'll get a clear notice saying so, and you can watch its status in Community → Under Review. Normal runs post instantly; approved big runs get a ✓ Verified badge.

Community Watch

See a stat that looks bogus? Open that operator and hit ⚑ Report. Reports are public but anonymous — just a count so the crew can judge for itself.

Proof & ✓ Verified

A raid an admin approves — or one you back with a screenshot/clip — earns a ✓ Verified badge on your profile and the raid History (uploaded images are discarded once reviewed). Skip proof on big runs too often and you'll land on the Liar, Liar board.

Quick FAQ

I logged a raid wrong — can it be fixed?
Yep. An admin can edit or remove any raid in Settings. Flag it in the chat and it'll get sorted — the board recalculates automatically.
Can I log from my phone?
Absolutely. The whole thing runs in your phone's browser — a lot of the crew logs right after they back out of a raid.
Why was my raid “held”?
It went over the board's max SR (or max op kills) per raid, so it's parked for a quick admin review before it counts — that's how we keep anyone from padding the board. You'll see a notice when it happens, and you can track it in Community → Under Review. Once approved it lands on your raid history with a ✓ Verified badge. No need to re-log it — logging it again won't speed things up.
Do my stats carry between seasons?
SR and standings reset each season, but your trophies and achievements history stick with you.

Ranks & deployment fee

Every rank below Iridescent splits into three divisions — I when you first climb in, up through III just before promotion. The deployment fee steps up a little with each division, so the higher you sit inside a rank, the more it costs to deploy. Iridescent is the apex live-ranked ladder — no divisions, one flat fee.

Hot & Cold Streaks New

The flames and frost next to operators on the leaderboard track your recent exfil form — a live read on whether you're on a heater or in a slump. It counts your most recent raids in a row and breaks the instant you flip: one clean exfil ends an ice streak, one death ends a flame. It's purely for bragging rights (and shame) — streaks don't change your SR.

🔥 On Fire — exfils in a row

Extract (get out alive) on back-to-back raids and you heat up: 2 in a row is a small flame, 4 a bigger flame, and 6+ a full inferno with fire sweeping across your row. Exfil is the hardest thing to do in DMZ, so a big flame is a genuine flex.

❄️ Ice Cold — no-exfils in a row

Die or leave empty-handed on back-to-back raids and you freeze over: 2 is a snowflake, 4 heavier frost, and 6+ hanging icicles with an icy glow on your row. Land a single exfil and you thaw right back out.
